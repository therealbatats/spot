Could SPHEREx really see Planet X?

Probably not. Planet Nine would be about magnitude 22–25 in visible light (Brown & Batygin 2016); SPHEREx reaches about 18.4 per channel after two full years (Crill et al. 2020). That gap is a factor of 25 or more (approximate: visible and infrared scales differ). Our dot is drawn far brighter so you can see how it moves. Sources: How it works.
